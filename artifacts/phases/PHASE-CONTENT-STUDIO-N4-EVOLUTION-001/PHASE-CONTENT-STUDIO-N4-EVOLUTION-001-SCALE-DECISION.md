# MCF Content Studio N4 Evolution — Scale Decision

## Decision

`APTO_PARA_ESCALA_CONTROLADA`

## What is approved

The N4 engine can be used as the baseline for producing additional lesson payloads without creating a bespoke composition for each lesson.

Evidence:
- architecture-heavy scale proof;
- UI/code-heavy scale proof;
- same `TechnicalLessonTemplate`;
- Video Lab V3 lesson authoring;
- 34-component library;
- registry/template discovery;
- browser/mobile validation;
- component matrix and audits.

## Conditions per lesson

Every lesson still requires:
- factual source validation;
- scene density/preflight;
- narration generation and synchronization;
- mobile visual QA;
- Review Lab or equivalent assistible review;
- final publication gate.

## Not approved

- automatic mass publication;
- treating silent Scale Proof MP4s as final lessons;
- bypassing asset/license/import governance;
- claiming real Figma integration until a concrete file/node is provided.

`MCF-CONTENT-STUDIO-N4-COURSE-SCALE-001`

Purpose:
use the validated engine to migrate/produce the remaining course lessons with narration and lesson-level Review Lab validation.
