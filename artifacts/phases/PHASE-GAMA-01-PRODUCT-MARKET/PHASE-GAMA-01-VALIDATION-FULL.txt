PHASE-GAMA-01 VALIDATION FULL

External evidence used:

1. McKinsey — The State of AI 2026
   - 40% of respondents from large organizations report scaling AI agents.
   - About two in ten organizations are scaling coding agents; 31% among large enterprises.
   - Organizations are using coding agents to build software internally.

2. Deloitte — Agentic AI is scaling faster than guardrails (2026)
   - survey of 3,235 IT and business leaders across 24 countries.
   - only 21% report mature agentic-AI governance.
   - gaps include decision boundaries, real-time monitoring and audit trails.

3. OneTrust — 2026 AI-Ready Governance Survey
   - 1,200 senior business decision-makers.
   - 87% encourage agent use; 47% report clear governance/oversight/controls.
   - only 5% report clear coordination and accountability across the AI lifecycle.

4. PwC — Trust and Safety Outlook 2026: AI agents as workforce counterparts
   - recommends verified identity, defined role, task-specific permissions, auditable records and risk-proportional human oversight.

5. LangChain — State of Agent Engineering 2026
   - 1,300+ respondents.
   - 57% report agents in production.
   - quality is a top production barrier for 32%.
   - observability is already common, reinforcing that observability alone is not a sufficient differentiation claim.

Competitive evidence:
- CrewAI: build/runtime plus enterprise governance, RBAC, audit trails, HITL, observability and multi-LLM.
- LangGraph: durable runtime, persistence, memory and HITL.
- Microsoft Agent Framework: agents, workflows, memory, telemetry, HITL and long-running execution.
- Temporal: durable execution, recovery and workflow history used in AI systems.

Inference discipline:
- Secondary market evidence supports existence of a governance/reliability problem.
- It does NOT prove demand specifically for MCF.
- It does NOT prove willingness-to-pay.
- It does NOT prove MCF has a durable moat.
- It does NOT substitute G5 pilot/customer validation.

Result:
The product thesis is credible enough to move forward, with external market validation still required before strong investor claims.
