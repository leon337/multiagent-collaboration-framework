# PHASE-GAMA-01 — Decisions

## D1 — Problem category
The MCF addresses controlled execution of agent teams, not basic agent creation.

## D2 — Primary ICP
Start with AI-native and engineering-platform teams already running multiple agents/models in software delivery workflows.

## D3 — Initial buyer
CTO / engineering leadership / AI-platform or platform-engineering leadership.

## D4 — Initial wedge
`Governed Autonomous Software Delivery` is the initial product wedge because it matches existing MCF evidence and the Gama Fund's Future of Software Development category.

## D5 — Market evidence classification
Secondary research strongly supports a gap between agent adoption and governance maturity. It does not prove demand or willingness-to-pay for MCF specifically.

## D6 — Competition
Competitive risk is `HIGH`. CrewAI, LangGraph, Microsoft Agent Framework and Temporal cover meaningful adjacent or overlapping capabilities.

## D7 — Differentiation discipline
The combination of authenticated authority, permission-bound execution, evidence-before-success, receipts, fail-closed claims, continuity and provider independence is a `DIFFERENTIATION_HYPOTHESIS`, not a proven moat.

## D8 — Business model
Open-source core plus paid control-plane/governance capabilities is a `HYPOTHESIS_ONLY` until packaging and willingness-to-pay are validated.

## D9 — G1 gate
`PASS_WITH_OPEN_MARKET_VALIDATION_GAP`.
Proceed to G2. Preserve G5 for real pilot/customer validation.
